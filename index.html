      questions.

      Is it free?

      Yes. During the beta everything is free. After that, Today, Grades, Calendar and up to 5 classes stay free. Pro will be $2.99 a month.

      What do I get with Pro?

      Unlimited classes, reminders, the study timer and study plan, backups and export, and themes. Free keeps Today, Grades, Calendar and up to 5 classes.

      When will Pro cost money?

      Not during the beta. Pro is not for sale yet, and you will never be charged unless you choose Pro yourself.

      Does it work with my school?

      Yes, any school. You type in your own schedule: Blue and Green days, A and B days, a three-day rotation or the same classes every day.

      Does it connect to StudentVUE, Schoology or Google Classroom?

      No. You type your grades and assignments yourself. That keeps your school login out of it, and it works the same at every school.

      Who can see my data?

      Only you. We do not sell it. Read the privacy page.

      Does it work on iPhone, Android and Chromebook?

      It runs in the browser, so it works on a phone, a Chromebook or a PC.

      Are the demos real?

      They use the same rules as the app, with sample data. The study timer and reminders are not in the app yet, so they are marked Coming soon.

      How old do I have to be?

      13 or older.

      When does it launch?

      No date yet. People on the waitlist get in first.
