Privacy

DRAFT. Plain-language placeholder. It needs a real legal review before launch.

Last updated: November 2026. Scool Tracker is made by one high school student.

Who can use it

Scool Tracker is free. You must be 13 or older. If you are under 13, do not use the app.

What the app stores

  • If you make an account: a scrambled login code made from your student ID (the student ID itself is never stored), plus the classes, tasks, grades and settings you type in.
  • If you start without an account (guest mode): everything stays only on that device. It is not saved on our servers.

Grades are typed by hand

Scool Tracker does not connect to StudentVUE, Google Classroom or any school account. You type your own grades. We never ask for your school login.

Who can see it

We do not sell your data. There are no ads, and we do not share it for ads. Keep your student ID to yourself: anyone who knows it can open your account.

Who stores it

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database host.

Delete your data

You can delete your account in the app under Settings. That removes your account data. You can also write to us. Contact: coming soon.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and the date above.
