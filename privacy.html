Privacy

DRAFT. Plain-language placeholder. It needs a real legal review before launch.

Last updated: November 2026. Scool Tracker is made by one high school student.

Who can use it

You must be 13 or older. If you are under 13, do not join the waitlist or use the app.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address. The form also asks for your grade and school. Right now only the email is saved.
  • In the app (when it launches): your account email and what you type in, like classes, assignments, grades and study time.

Grades are typed by hand

Scool Tracker does not connect to StudentVUE, Google Classroom or any school account. You type your own grades. We never ask for your school login.

Who can see it

Your app data is visible only to you. We do not sell your data. We do not share it for ads.

Who stores it

Data is stored with Supabase, our database host.

Waitlist emails

We use your email to tell you when your spot opens. Ask us to delete it any time.

Delete your data

Write to us and we will delete your waitlist email and any account data. Contact: coming soon.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and the date above.
